In today's fast-paced world, the traditional telephone order of food has become inconvenient to customers and the food delivery store. The online food ordering system provides convenience for customers. It overcomes the disadvantages of the traditional queuing system. This Android application increases the takeout of foods among visitors. Therefore, this system enhances the speed and standardization of taking orders from customers. It provides a better communication platform. The user details are noted electronically. Using this application, the customers need not go to the restaurant by themselves, but they can order the meals through Android mobiles anywhere. In this system, there are four, namely, admin, delivery boy, restaurant manager, and user. Admins can login, manage restaurants by adding, updating, and deleting them, and manage delivery personnel by adding, updating, and deleting them. Admin can also check registered users and the total count of orders. Delivery boys can login and see the allotted orders; they can upload the status of the order, whether it is picked up, on the way, or delivered. Restaurant managers can login and update their restaurant details; they can even check for reviews and ratings given by users. They can manage the menu by adding new items and deleting unwanted ones. The manager can manage orders by allotting them to the delivery boy and can update the status of delivery. The manager can see the payment done in electronic mode. Users can register and log in. Users have the option to choose the cuisine and hotels nearby. The user will get details about restaurants, like their name, location, and reviews. Users can select the food from the menu list, add it to their favorites, and get it processed further. The user can view the history of their orders and the current order status. The user has online payment options. The user will get a notification of the order status.
